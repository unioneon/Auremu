Auremu 0.1.0 — Windows x64 preview

Run Auremu.cmd or bin\Auremu.exe. Extract the complete portable ZIP first.
On first launch accept the Android SDK terms and use Install Android.
The wizard downloads official SDK Tools, Temurin JDK 21, Emulator, ADB,
and the Android 17 Google Play x86_64 system image. Internet and 15 GB
of free space are required. Enable Windows Hypervisor Platform (WHPX)
and CPU virtualization, then restart Windows if requested.

Portable data: data\ beside bin\ and runtime\.
Installed data: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Auremu\data.
No personal Android device, account or saved session is included.
Removing the installed app preserves Android data.

Downloads and instructions: https://github.com/unioneon/Auremu
This is an unsigned preview build; no performance guarantee is made.
Google Play and Google services have their own terms and data practices.
